# Syracuse Children EBLL CT 2020 2025

Elevated blood lead levels among tested children, by census tract, 2020-2025 in City of Syracuse. Percent of tested children with blood lead levels of ≥ 5 mcg/dL. This data set was created by joining testing data (extracted from from https://onondaga.gov/health-lead/mapping-lead-poisoning/ on 2026-09-17) to 2020 Census Tracts (2020 Census\CensusTracts_Syracuse_2020_Project) by census tract name. Source data notes included below.---Data represent tested children under 18 years of age.Location data represent address provided at the time of testing.Includes unconfirmed fingerstick tests.Excludes tests with unmatched address information.Census Tracts with fewer than 10 children tested were suppressed.Census Tracts according to the U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Decennial Census.Data are based on small numbers and should be interpreted with caution.2025 data are provisional.
