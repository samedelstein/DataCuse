Full publisher description

Hourly temperature data readings from Syracuse's Road temperature sensors. These have been placed at the locations below. These locations were picked because they are some of the steepest roads in Syracuse and the Department of Public Works wanted to track the temperature of the roads to help monitor when the roads may have frozen with ice during the winter season.
