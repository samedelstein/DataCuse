# Greater Syracuse Bike Suitability Ratings (2020)

Bike Suitability Ratings were collected by SMTC staff in 2019. You can access the app built by SMTC at Bike Suitability Map of Greater Syracuse (2020) . Disclaimer: The data is for planning purposes only, and the SMTC does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of the information. Label Definition Bike Suitability Ratings: Excellent: Highly Recommend Slow moving traffic; low volume traffic & some separation from vehicles. Good: Acceptable Moderately traveled with some separation from vehicles traveling at slower speeds. Fair: Urban Acceptable Moderately traveled with little separation from vehicles traveling at slower speeds; or roads with higher volumes of traffic and some separation from vehicles. Poor: Not suitable Heavily traveled with fast moving traffic, little to no separation from vehicles, and/or rough cycling conditions.
