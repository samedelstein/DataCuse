Data Dictionary: Label Definition Definition Source Fund_Name: The type of fund where the expenses and revenues are tracked. Office of Management and Budget Dept_Name: The department, agency or category associated with the revenue. Office of Management and Budget Account: The account number and name as outlined in the City’s financial tracking system. Office of Management and Budget Budget: The amount (in dollars) that the City budgeted for anticipated revenue. A negative number reflects an adjustment to other associated revenues.
