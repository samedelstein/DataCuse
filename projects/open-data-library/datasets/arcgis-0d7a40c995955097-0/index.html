Office of Management and Budget Dept_Name: The department, agency or category associated with the expenditure. Office of Management and Budget Account_Name: The account number and name as outlined in the City’s financial tracking system. Office of Management and Budget Budget: The amount (in dollars) that the City budgeted for anticipated expenditure. A negative number reflects an adjustment to other associated expenditures.
