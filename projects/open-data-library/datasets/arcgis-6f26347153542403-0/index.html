Description: Parcel file reflecting all tax map parcels in the City of Syracuse joined to parcel-level data from multiple city departments including Assessment (property type, parcel dimensions, ownership information, year built), Water (water activity), Neighborhood and Business Development (Code Enforcement monitored vacant structures, geographic designations, zoning designations, historic and locally protected sites). Parcel geometry provided by Onondaga County Planning. Dataset Contact Information: Organization: City of Syracuse Position: Data Program Manager City: Syracuse, NY E-Mail Address: opendata@syr.gov
