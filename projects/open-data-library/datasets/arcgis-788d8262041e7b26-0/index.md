# Syracuse Public Art NEW

A list of public art that is currently installed on City owned property or areas in the City of Syracuse. Data is maintained through the Syracuse Public Arts Commission (SPAC). Data includes the type of public art (whether it is a sculpture, painting, mural, etc.), what type of material the public art is made from, the name of the artist(s), and other information. If you are a property owner who has a mural or public art on your building or property, and you would like information about that public art added to our dataset, please let us know at opendata@syr.gov For more information about the City of Syracuse Public Art Ordinance and Commission, contact: publicart@syr.gov . Data Dictionary: Label Definition Definition Source Title Title of the Public Art Piece. Syracuse Public Arts Commission (SPAC) Type The type of public art (Sculpture, Monument, Mural, Mosaic, etc.) SPAC Address The address, intersection, or building that the public art is located at. SPAC Latitude The latitude of the geographic location of the public art. SPAC Longitude The longitude of the geographic location of the public art. SPAC Artist Last The last name of the primary artist(s) who created the public art piece. SPAC Artist First The first name of the primary artist(s) who created the public art piece. SPAC Additional Artists Additional artist(s), if there were any, who helped create the public art piece. SPAC Media The type of material that was used to make the public art piece (Steel, Bronze, Granit, Painted Cement, Concrete, Steel, etc.) SPAC Date Created The year that the public art piece was created. SPAC Date Erected The year that the public art piece was put where it installed. SPAC TNT Area The Tomorrow's Neighborhoods Today section of the city that this public art piece is located within. SPAC Neighborhood The Syracuse city neighborhood that this public art piece is located within. SPAC Specific Location A text description of where the public art piece is located in relation to the address location. SPAC Additional Information Other information about this public art piece, such as what the public art piece is about, why it was commissioned, or about the artist.
