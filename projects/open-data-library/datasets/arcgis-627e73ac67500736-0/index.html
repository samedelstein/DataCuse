Data Dictionary: Label Definition Definition Source Fund.Name The type of fund where the expenses are tracked. Office of Management and Budget Dept.Name The department associated with the expense. Office of Management and Budget Account.Name The account number and name as outlined in the City’s financial tracking system. Office of Management and Budget Amount The amount (in dollars) that the City spent on the expenditure. A negative number reflects an adjustment in the amount spent on the expenditure. Office of Management and Budget Account Description Description of the amount that the spending was pulled from. Office of Managment and Budget DepartmentGroup The department, agency, or category associated with the expenditure.
