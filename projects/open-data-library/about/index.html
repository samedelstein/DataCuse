Methods & limits

A second door to public data.

DataCuse is an independent project. This library helps people find public city sources beyond the official open data portal.

What counts as a dataset here?

One ArcGIS layer or table, or one extracted document table. Service containers, subjects and record counts are different units. Related editions are grouped by family in the machine catalog; they can overlap.

How we check the official catalog

We compare the official DCAT feed with public service URLs, item IDs and layer IDs. We normalize URL casing and encoding. A whole-service link is treated conservatively as a match. Document downloads are checked by URL. Similar topics under different names can still exist; no-match results are not proof of exclusivity or deliberate withholding.

What is reviewed?

Selected ArcGIS sources have inspected fields and live record counts; coverage can still be undocumented. The full directory adds metadata-only sources. CSV extracts preserve source values and record their original checksum, workbook sheet or PDF page, plus validation results. Source files are included for comparison.

Three extraction families

  • Budget workbooks: seven fund sheets per edition. Values stay separate by fiscal year and Actual, Budget, Estimate or Proposed. Cached totals and detail coexist; do not sum all rows. City Rates remains in the original workbook.
  • Water report: Tables 8, 19 and 20 from the 2025 report. Units, site labels and reported annual averages are preserved. Other tables remain in the PDF.
  • CRB: all 25 CSV members of the October 2026 archive. Blank index headers become source_row_index; other values, including NA, stay intact. Related summaries overlap.

What an update means

The directory was checked 2026-11-01. File-specific check dates appear on dataset pages. Refreshes are manual. A fresh scan produces candidate data and a change report; it cannot publish the site automatically. Unknown document checksums stop extraction until the changed source is reviewed.

If a source disappears, its page and last reviewed metadata remain with an availability notice. If it enters the official catalog, it leaves the default collection. An access failure is not called a deletion. We do not know every dataset that ever existed.

Use and attribution

Public access does not by itself establish a reuse license. Consult the original publisher’s terms; no blanket license is asserted for city data. DataCuse’s extraction is identified separately from the city source. No login, analytics, API key or hosted model is required.
