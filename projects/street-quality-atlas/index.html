Method

Property Atlas, rotated toward the street.

The workflow uses the existing parcel SQLite database and Google Street View image pipeline, then derives a road-axis heading from OpenStreetMap so the camera looks along the street instead of at the house.

  • Source database: Syracuse Property Atlas parcels.
  • Camera heading: nearest OpenStreetMap road segment, with parcel-neighbor fallback.
  • Vision chain: Gemini 3.5, Gemini 2.5, then local Ollama when configured.
  • Outputs: street images, object observations, condition assessments, issue boxes, CSV, and Label Studio-ready JSON.
