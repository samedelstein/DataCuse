Street Quality Atlas

A street-facing companion to the Syracuse Property Atlas: same parcel database, same civic data frame, but aimed down the road to catalog pavement, curbs, sidewalks, signs, hydrants, and other public-right-of-way conditions.

Method

Same parcels, street-facing camera.

The workflow keeps the EveryLot rhythm but changes the target: use the address or parcel coordinate to find Street View, set an explicit road heading, capture both directions where possible, then analyze the street and sidewalk instead of the building facade.

01

Capture

Generate Google Street View images with explicit headings so the camera looks along the road outside each address.

02

Catalog

Record hydrants, curbs, signs, sidewalks, storm drains, pavement markings, and visible maintenance issues.

03

Review

Store normalized bounding boxes and export CSV or Label Studio-ready tasks for human review and correction.
