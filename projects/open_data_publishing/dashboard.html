      What the numbers mean.

      What counts as a dataset?

      Annual editions, hosted views, and replacement items count separately. Download formats do not. Service-directory counts use containers, not unique subjects.

      Excluded catalog entries
      Which dates are used?

      Creation means the ArcGIS item's timestamp, not necessarily its first public release. The publication measure uses formal publication, then formal creation, then item creation. Item timestamps use UTC; unzoned formal dates retain their written date.

      Data-edit age uses the layer's editingInfo.dataLastEditDate. Zero or missing values are unknown. It measures an edit, not record coverage or refresh reliability.

      Can this show all creations, including deleted items?

      No. Both date measures use the current catalog. The historical-link sample cannot fill the gaps: it is incomplete, mixes item types, and usually lacks creation dates. Archived page timestamps found in this review describe the portal itself, not the individual datasets.

      A complete history would need creation, sharing, and deletion records from the city, potentially including older saved exports. Future snapshots can record changes observed between checks.

      What do older accounts establish?

      The city launched the portal in 2017 and reported 29 additions during 2022 and early 2023. A 2024 interview described more than 100 datasets. These support expansion, but their counting methods differ. They are not an annual inventory series.
