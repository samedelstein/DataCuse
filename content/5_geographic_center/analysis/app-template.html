What counts as the center? The area-weighted centroid of the full Syracuse municipal boundary, including water inside the city limits: . Imagine balancing a flat cardboard cutout of the city.

Distances are approximate straight-line miles, not walking or driving routes. Bearings are measured clockwise from true north. The optional phone compass is approximate; sensors and magnetic north can introduce error. The calculated point is not a verified visitor destination.

Location calculations happen on your device. This app does not send or save your coordinates, load third-party maps, or use an IP-based location. Your browser or operating system may use its own location service when you grant permission.
